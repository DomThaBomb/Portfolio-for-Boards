Genre Discussion: Multiplayer - Versus

What defines a good game in this genre

Halo 3: The game has the sense of balance between the skill and luck when it comes to it's levels and gameplay as well (even if almost all of the elements are skill-based. The online encounters can get pretty lucky whether bad or good). It's a simple, yet fun multiplayer experience that does not feel feature creeped to death like from most FPS games nowadays. Most FPS's tend to want to add a certain feature that end up being a better version of that feature Taking for instance, Call of Duty.

Amongst the Best Examples

Halo Reach:

Mostly due to the fact that it's pretty balanced and does not feel featured creeped. By most people becoming players to the game, this seems to be one of the best in the franchise well-round. It shows that the best of the best games tend to be from the ones that are the most simple to pick up and understand and play through to enjoy.

Super Smash Bros. Melee

This was one of the games that brought in a really good definition of a good sequal when it first came out. Being one of the greater games in the franchise, the game brought about many roster of characters that can be played unlike it's prvious game to the different hardware on the Nintendo 64. This time, bringing it to the Gamecube: Another game system that would be part of its' rivalry to 2 other brand consoles of the playstation 2 and the Original Xbox. This game has spawned in numorous of rivalry competitions that still go on today, a separate modded game that inspired to create a sequal to this game with it's timeless mechanics and techniques related to it.

Minecraft

This is a different case in that the community really cares about the content and the servers that have been made over the years. Making for some historical feets/moments in the process and create tons of separate communities that would all see the game differently.

Not-so great examples

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2

While the franchise throughout the years has seen a run of it's great games, one of the things that has never ceased to fail is the broken part of every one of them. One Man Army Secondary slot weapon item would be a prime example of this in Modern Warfare 2. At that point, there are granade launchers, a RPG and a Javelin into the game. Majorly, the granade launcher is a primary attachment to any primary weapon. This led to a design flaw within the creating class system in which that you can replenish both the ammo of your gun AND the attachment ammo. Meaning that as long as you were alive, you could mostly just chuck them over the map and get easy kills almost guarenteed at start of the game.

Battlefield

Meant to be a contenter and rival against Call of Duty during its' prime years, It's main appeal tends to be it's big map design and large scale battle combat. While this is pretty cool as a concept, in practice and seeing how these games play, they tend to feel more like open worlds from each map presented. One of the only great things that can be seen from this game is the sense of it being a military sim. It gets you in that feeling of being more of a solider in combat than Call of Duty tends to have in some of its games.
