    Postmordem

    The concept for this map in particular was to have it be more like an arena-style map with some equal distributions of combat from each corner.

    In constant playing for research, most of the maps are maze-like and are relatively small enough to slide around. The smaller the room to slide, the greater the skill gap there is relative to the map.

    The idea for this map was to have it be a very simple and small map that is consistent to the point that there is no time wasted when playing. if you see the player, kill the player. If you don't, pick the other spot. Rinse and repeat.

    Project Break the Targets (Super Smash Bros. Crusade)

    Speedrun Video

    Level Design

    This was a 16-level project that I was working on for each day for the past 4 weeks.

    The focus for the maps was to give a unique experience similar to that of Super Smash Bros Melee's Break the Targets.

    The maps support the character's unique special abilities that not any other character would otherwise do.

    Experiences with the game

    The game that I worked on was a modded version of Super Smash Bros. Crusade. The mod being CMC+ V8 in specific. Since that they added a feature in CMC+ recently of a level editor for both BTT and BTP, I decided to that I had the potential to create some.

    The gamemode that I chose would be Break the Targets, since that I have world-class experience with it at the time (I have since retired from the excessive nature of playing it, but do play it ever so often and get decent records).

    Program and Speedrun video

    The program used for this is GameMaker 8.1. An engine that goes as far back as 2011. And it's the one that held the features necessary for the project. It's a simple drag and drop for what it was set up to do.
